The Healthy Plate

A healthy plate is a simple visual guide that helps you portion your food for a balanced meal.

Half the Plate: Vegetables and Fruits

  • Fill half your plate with colorful vegetables and fruits.
  • Choose mostly non-starchy vegetables like spinach, carrots, broccoli, or cabbage.
  • Limit potatoes and French fries, as they do not count as healthy vegetables.

One-Quarter of the Plate: Whole Grains

  • Fill one-quarter of your plate with healthy, whole-grain carbohydrates.
  • Pick options like brown rice, whole-wheat pasta, quinoa, or oats.
  • Limit refined grains like white rice and white bread.

One-Quarter of the Plate: Healthy Protein

  • Fill the final quarter with a good protein source.
  • Choose fish, poultry, beans, eggs, tofu, or nuts.
  • Limit red meat and avoid processed meats like bacon or cold cuts.

Healthy Fats and Drinks

  • Use healthy cooking oils like olive or canola oil in small amounts.
  • Drink water, tea, or coffee with little to no sugar.
